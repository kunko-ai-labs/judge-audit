## 1. Transfer tests

Items split in two halves by item (seed 2026, 1,487 / 1,487; an item's three languages on the same side). On one half the English rows validate a threshold at a 5 % target error (`coverage_at_risk`, exact one-sided 95 %, the walk starting at the cut that validates with 2 errors); on the other half the target-language rows at or above it are counted. The halves swap and the counts are pooled. One-sided 95 % lower and upper bounds on the pooled error, and p, the binomial P(errors ≥ observed | 5 %) on the pooled count (equivalent to the lower bound), are **approximate**: each half's count is binomial given the threshold chosen on the other half, but the two halves are the same items in two languages, so they depend on each other through the thresholds and the pooled count is not one binomial (the cross-fit approximation of docs/judges.md; amendment A5). Holm rests on these p. **Resolved** when the Holm-adjusted p across the 6 tests is below 0.05, with m = 6 fixed (a test not run counts as not resolved). A half on which English validates no threshold admits no row; the test is not run only when neither half validates one. "Lower at its Holm level" is the pooled lower bound at the one-sided level of the test's Holm step (α / 6 for the smallest p, then α / 5, …), next to the unadjusted 95 % bound, up to the first step that fails: Holm stops there, and no later bound is given. A row without a confidence is never above a threshold. The English control is the English rows of the same halves above the same thresholds.

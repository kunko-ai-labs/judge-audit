## Rank agreement and the latent ρ (part B)

Spearman correlation (average ranks) of two read-outs' confidences on the rows **both** got right. ρ is the latent correlation that reproduces it: one fixed sample of 100,000 standard normal pairs (seed 2026), the second coordinate mixed as ρ·z₁ + √(1−ρ²)·z₂, each cut into its method's tie shares on those same rows (one with no ties on these rows is not cut), bisection on ρ ∈ [0, 0.999] for 40 steps; a correlation at or below the ρ = 0 value maps to 0. The ties cap the rank correlation the map can reach ("map max", its value at the top of the range): an observed value at or above it is **saturated**, its ρ is only a lower bound, and a proposed ρ is capped at 0.95. Interval: the 2.5 % and 97.5 % percentiles of 2,000 bootstrap resamples (seed 2026) of those rows, each mapped the same way (resamples where the correlation is undefined are counted, not used). The rows both got wrong are reported, not used.
